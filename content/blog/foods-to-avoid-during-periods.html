Foods to Avoid During Periods

Menstrual cycles are a common and regular aspect of a woman’s life. However, the cycle leads to much physical discomfort every month. Stomach cramps, bloating, fatigue, mood swings, bowel problems, etc, are some of the most common issues during menstrual discharge. Most menstrual and premenstrual problems source back to hormonal imbalance. However, few dietary intakes can make a crucial difference to your level of comfort and discomfort. Avoid some food elements during periods to get maximum comfort.

Food you should stay away from during periods:

Salty food

Food that contains too much salt, often has a high index of sodium in it. High sodium content food can lead to indigestion and bloating. Women already experience bloating during their menstrual cycle. Hence, you must avoid salty food during your periods.

Sweet food

Mood swings are common during period cycles. Many feel that having sweet food can make your mood swings better. However, this is a myth. Often, when you have too much sweet, there is a sugar spike in your blood, which is followed by a sugar crash. A sudden blood sugar crash is when women can feel all the more down and depressed. Hence, it is okay to have sweet items in moderation during your periods, but having too many sweets is not a good idea.

Deep fried food

Deep-fried foods often have an extra amount of oil in them. Consuming too much oily food during your periods can lead to increased gastrointestinal issues. Deep-fried food can irritate your stomach lining and lead to gas and bloating. Top dieticians in Gurgaon will advise you to avoid too much deep-fried food as it can lead to indigestion and acidity.

Caffeine intake

Coffee and tea are two of the most popular hot beverages consumed worldwide. However, too much caffeine can lead to bloating and diarrhea. However, if you are already used to caffeine, withdrawing from it can lead to irritation, headaches, and fatigue. Hence, your caffeine intake must be regulated if not stopped.

Alcohol intake

Alcohol can lead to a feel-good effect in the person who consumes the drink. Many women feel that alcohol can be an effective way to cut down on body tiredness, and depression during periods. However, this is a wrong idea. Alcohol consumption can make matters worse. Too much alcohol can make you dehydrated and can increase your feelings of depression and anxiety. Even the sleep you get after consuming alcohol is not a normal one, and you will feel worse once you get up from your sleep. The hangover from the alcohol can make you feel worse and can increase your depression. You must always avoid alcohol during periods.

Spicy food

Spicy food is again a comforting idea for many, but it can be harmful for your systems during your periods. Spicy food can lead to gastrointestinal issues and can lead to stomach issues and bloating. Spicy food can create more gastrointestinal problems, and can make things much worse for you. Stay away from spicy food during your periods to have stable and healthy periods.

Red Meat

Red meat helps to generate more prostaglandins. Prostaglandins can lead to greater stomach cramps and can make your periods all the more painful. Further red meat can lead to constipation and when cooked with a lot of spice can be difficult to digest. Stay away from red meat during your periods. Switch to chicken, fish, and turkey, instead as a healthy protein source.

The food you cannot tolerate

Each one of us has some food items that make us uncomfortable. Make a list of such food which makes you uncomfortable, and you must stay away from them for good during your periods. To keep food you are intolerant to, is a simple solution to have safe and comfortable periods.

Stay hydrated

You must remain well-hydrated during your periods. Many women feel that to avoid going to the bathroom more they must have less water. This is one of the sore mistakes you can make. Have adequate water and all other forms of healthy liquids. Include fruit juices, natural coconut water, and other forms of healthy liquids to stay as well-hydrated as possible.

Rest well

Another crucial step to stay healthy during periods is to rest well. You must follow a healthy sleeping pattern and must sleep for at least eight hours a day. Proper rest and sleep can make your periods all tempore smooth and comfortable.

Take care

Periods are one of the most regular aspects for women. Your periods should be as routine as possible. Regular periods are a sign of proper health. However, you must practice mindful eating and eat things that will keep you comfortable and happy throughout your periods. Read more on the topic of choosing healthy food. Talk to women who have investigated the matter to get helpful tips. Increase your awareness for safe and comfortable periods.
