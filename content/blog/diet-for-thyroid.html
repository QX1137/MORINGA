The thyroid gland plays a vital role in the body's functioning through hormone production. Whether it is for regulating body temperature, controlling metabolism, mood management, muscle control or brain development, this small gland ensures all the functions of the body work properly. Specifically, when metabolic functions are discussed, it is the process where the food is converted into energy to make the digestive system better. The butterfly-shaped small organ, the thyroid gland is a part of the endocrine system, that maintains the hormonal balance to regulate bodily functions. But, improper thyroid functioning can lead to several health disorders.

Primarily, two types of thyroid disorders, namely, hyperthyroidism and hypothyroidism may be caused due to several conditions. From iodine deficiency, congenital hypothyroidism, and Hashimoto’s disease to thyroid nodules, conditions leading to thyroid disease, need to be reversed rather than accepting them as a permanent health disorder.
