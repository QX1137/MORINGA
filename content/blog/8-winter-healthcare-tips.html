  1. Winter-care and properly dressing up to stay protected from cold

    2. Wrap yourself with the snuggle of soft woollen clothes or layer up warmth by choosing the proper winter, the whole idea is to protect yourself in the winter.   2. Hydration of the skin and lungs is a must

    3. As an extension of the previous tip, you need to keep your skin hydrated or moisturized even when the full-sleeves’ woollen garments are being worn on a daily basis.
