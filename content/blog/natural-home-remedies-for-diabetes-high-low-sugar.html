What exactly is Diabetes?

Diabetes is a condition that makes it difficult for the body to metabolize blood glucose, most commonly known as blood sugar. If not addressed on time, high blood sugar levels can raise the risk of cardiovascular conditions like stroke and heart disease. There are primarily two types of Diabetes, namely Type 1 Diabetes and Type 2 Diabetes.

The treatment for both two forms of Diabetes differs. It is also important to note that Diabetes is not always the result of leading an unhealthy lifestyle; sometimes, it could be genetic as well.
